Our Story

Father and Son

East Point is a family business founded by Paul Andriopoulos in 2001. We provide labels, packaging, and print to businesses across Nova Scotia. Paul has been working in the print industry since the 80s. Thirty years later and we continue to support many of the same clients since day one. Peter, Paul's son, frequently on the road between Halifax and Cape Breton, has been running the day-to-day since 2016.

Our Process

Built on Trust

Our trade-only manufacturers do not directly employ a sales force. They focus on producing your goods with utmost care and maximum efficiency — true specialists in production. They rely on partners like us to develop relationships, treat people honestly, and deliver their formidable production capabilities to the market.

What we bring to the table is in-depth knowledge of manufacturing processes, materials, and common workflow issues related to printing. We know exactly where to take your job to ensure your requirements are met. We don't own equipment, we don't carry stock, and have no layers of management — this lack of overhead allows us to pass on low prices to you. You don't pay for our advice, guidance, support — only the printed goods — that's how we've worked for the last 25 years and we don't plan on changing.

Beyond communicating your vision, needs, and budget — you won't have to think about the printed aspects of your business. You end up with an exceptional product, designed to your taste, with all of the small details and logistics looked after. Your decision to work with us will leave you with affordable products and more time to focus on what you do best.
