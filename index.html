Our Story

Father and Son

East Point is a family business founded by Paul Andriopoulos in 2001. We provide labels, packaging, and print to businesses across Nova Scotia. Paul has been working in the print industry since the 80s. Thirty years later and we continue to support some of the same clients. Peter, Paul's son, frequently on the road between Halifax and Cape Breton, has been running the day-to-day since 2016.

Our Story

Two People Sharing the Expertise of Many

We represent a handful of production facilities across Canada — each possessing its own set of strengths and capabilities. Once again, some of these relationships have been nurtured for more than thirty years. We tip our hat to our partners — they are incredibly good at what they do. Our job is to bring this wide array of capabilities and expertise right to our customer's doorstep. We match your needs with the right facility and make sure your goods are produced properly, at a fair price, and in a timely manner.

We're pretty straightforward about how we do business. We answer the phone, call people back when we say we will, and treat your business like it's our own. Nothing complicated. Just good people, great partners, and a commitment to doing what we said we'd do.

Our Products

We work with the best manufacturers in the country to provide a wide array of printed materials. If you don't see exactly what you're looking for below, just give us a call. Chances are we can help you out. If we can't, we'll tell you that — and more than likely be able to point you in the right direction.

Labels

  • A wide variety of substrates
  • Freezer, moisture, heat & temperature-resistant options
  • BRCGS-certified solutions available
  • Short-run and high-volume production
  • Flexographic and digital printing
  • Variable data and personalization
  • Premium embellishments and finishes — foil, holographic, UV varnish, etc.
  • Expanded content / multi-layer labels
  • Materials and adhesives engineered for specific applications
  • Fast turnaround times

Folding Cartons

  • A wide variety of board stocks and weights
  • Recycled and sustainable kraft options
  • Custom structural styles — tuck-end, sleeve, rigid, and more
  • Short-run and high-volume production
  • Offset, digital, and UV-cured printing
  • Embossing, foil, and metallic finishes
  • Precision die-cutting and multi-level embossing
  • Flat-pack designs built for automated filling
  • Tight registration and consistent color across runs
  • Fast turnaround times

Flexible Packaging

  • Stand-up pouches
  • Flow wrap and pillow pouches
  • Stick packs
  • Spout pouches
  • Sachets and single-serve packs
  • Quad-seal pouches with valve
  • Rollstock and film for form-fill-seal
  • Barrier protection against moisture, oxygen & light
  • Matte, gloss & metallic finishes
  • Recyclable and compostable film options

Print

  • Business cards, including folded and specialty stocks
  • Brochures and flyers
  • Postcards and direct mail pieces
  • Booklets and notebooks
  • Business forms and cheques
  • Envelopes, in a range of formats
  • Banners, signage, and large format
  • Tickets and numbered forms
  • Variable data and personalization
  • Short-run and digital production
